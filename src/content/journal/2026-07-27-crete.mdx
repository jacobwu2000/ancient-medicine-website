On the 27th I flew from Athens to Heraklion, the capital of Crete where the ancient city of Knossos was located. I visited both the Knossos Palace and the Heraklion Archaeological Museum. The museum contains many iconic artworks from the Minoan Civilization, including the Bull-Leaping Fresco, the Prince of the Lilies, and the Dolphin Fresco. Not much is known about Minoan medicine (Linear A has yet to be deciphered), though the poppy goddess figurines are evidence of opium use for religious or potentially healing purposes.

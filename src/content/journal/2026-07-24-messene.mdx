The Asclepieion in Messene was situated at the heart of the city adjacent to the Agora. Some of the interesting structures in Messene's Asclepieion complex include the Arsinoe fountain house, named for the mother of Asclepius (according to the Messenians at least); the Artemision, said to have housed Damophon's colossal marble statue of the goddess; and the Sebasteion, a cult sanctuary for the Roman emperors. Much of our knowledge of the site comes from Pausanias' *Description of Greece*.

In the summer of 2026, I traveled to Greece for a month. For the first two weeks, I participated in Paideia's *Living Greek in Greece* program. I arrived in Athens on 7/12 and parted ways with the amazing faculty and students there in Nafplio. Overall, I had an amazing time; I brushed up on my Ancient Greek, I tasted iconic Greek food, and of course, I got to see the timeless sites and learn about their history. Some standouts from my trip include hiking up the Acrocorinth (my calves died), watching a modern retelling of Aristophanes' very vulgar *Peace* at the Ancient Theater of Epidaurus (we were unfortunately interrupted by pouring rain), and writing my own mini-skit in Ancient Greek (*[PLACEHOLDER: Ancient Greek title — the original didn't survive a copy-paste encoding mishap; retype it here]* coming soon to theaters near you).

After the program, I decided to visit some sites on my own for research purposes. I visited sites in Messene, Epidaurus, Argos, Corinth, Crete, Kos, Trikala, and Athens, in that order. The goal was to investigate various sites related to ancient healing. The entries that follow are a brief record of what I saw, what I learned, and what I found interesting at each stop.

# Theory or Observation? The *Epidemics* Constitutions and *Airs, Waters, Places*

<p class="lede">A close reading of the four “constitutions” of the Hippocratic <em>Epidemics</em> against the environmental theory of <em>Airs, Waters, Places</em>.</p>

<aside class="question-box">
  <p class="question-box__label">Working question</p>
  <p>
    Read closely against <em>Airs, Waters, Places</em>, do the constitutions
    (<em>katastaseis</em>) of <em>Epidemics</em> I and III look like its environmental
    theory <em>applied</em> to one place over time, or like the raw observation that
    such a theory could have been built from? And how do a few case histories sit
    inside the constitutions they belong to?
  </p>
</aside>
